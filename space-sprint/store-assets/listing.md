## Short description (one line)
Race your rocket along a glowing highway high above the clouds: jump the gaps, hit the boost pads and beat 4 rival pilots!

## Full description
Blast off in Rocket Skyway, a fast and colourful rocket racer on a sky-high highway floating above the clouds!

Hold the gas, steer between lanes and jump over gaps and barriers as you race 4 rival pilots to the finish line. Hit green boost pads for super speed, bounce off yellow jump pads, steer around red hazard pads and refuel on blue pads before your tank runs dry. Your friendly buddy pilot races right beside you and cheers you on, while the other rivals bump you, overtake you and shout when you pass them!

New to racing? A quick TRAINING run teaches you everything step by step, with slow motion before your first jumps.

Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next level and earn up to 3 stars. Collect coins to unlock 6 rockets in the Hangar, including the classic SKYHAWK 07 and the Rainbow rocket. Then try Endless mode: how far can you fly?

**Features**
- 12 levels across 4 worlds: Moon, Mars, Nebula and Ice Rings
- Race 4 rival pilots, plus a friendly buddy who stays close
- Floaty low-gravity jumps, BIG AIR and MEGA AIR bonuses
- Boost pads, jump pads, barriers, hazard pads and fuel to manage
- The view widens as you speed up, with streaks and sparks
- Quick guided TRAINING for new players
- Endless mode with your best distance saved
- 6 rockets to unlock; progress saves automatically
