# Store listing: Chain Riders

## Short description (one line)
Race, punch and KICK your way through a gang of rival bikers in this retro combat motorcycle racer!

## Full description
Hit the throttle and fight dirty! Chain Riders is a fast, retro-style motorcycle racer where the rules are simple: finish in the top 3, by any means necessary.

- 🏍️ Race against 7 rival bikers who punch back, taunt you and hold grudges
- 🦶 KICK rivals into traffic or off the road for bonus cash
- 👊 Chain punches together for COMBOS
- ⛓️ Knock out armed riders and steal their chains and clubs
- ⚡ Nail the PERFECT START for a boost off the line
- 🌴 Four tracks: Pacific Coast, Desert Run, Neon City and Alpine Pass
- 🔧 Spend your winnings on engine, armor and frame upgrades

## Controls
Keyboard: ↑ gas · ↓ brake · ← → steer · SPACE kick · Z punch left · C punch right · P pause · M sound
Mobile: gas is automatic · ◀ ▶ steer · KICK / PUNCH buttons · BRAKE
